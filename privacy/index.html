Privacy

Travi keeps your travel history on your iPhone first. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in.

Photos

Travi reads photo dates and locations on your device to suggest trips. Photos are never uploaded. Receipt text is read on your device.

When you sign in

Your places, trips, profile and the groups you join are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt (EU) so friends can see what you choose to share and your data survives a new phone. Friends never see your email, wishlist or notes.

Deleting

Settings → Delete account removes everything Travi holds about you in the cloud. What is on your phone stays yours.
